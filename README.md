## ⚙️ **Detailed Features**

### **🎯 Aimbot**
- **FOV (20–600px):** Limits target acquisition angle.
- **Smoothness (1–20):** Controls how quickly the aim adjusts.
- **Hitbox Priorities:**
  - **Nearest:** Prioritizes the closest hitbox (faster reaction).
  - **Priority:** Switches to head for precise shots.
- **Filters:**
  - **Aim at souls:** Targets dropped souls (for quick farming).
  - **Aim at trooper:** Targets enemy creeps.
- **Behind the walls:** Ignores obstacles (disable in open areas).

### **👁️ ESP**
- **Drag & Drop Customization:**
  - **Box:** Adjust fill, outline, gradient colors.
  - **Health bar:** Shows enemy HP.
  - **Level:** Displays hero progression gaps.
  - **Nickname:** Player names.
  - **Distance:** Range to target.
  - **Shop zone:** Highlights item shop areas (common fight zones).
- **Extras:**
  - **Only visible:** Renders only visible targets.
  - **Skeleton:** Shows enemy posture (running/aiming).
  - **Particle:** Highlights Nexus markers on the map.

### **🔫 Weapon & Combat**
- **Recoil Compensation:**
  - **Reactive mode:** Adapts to real-time recoil.
  - **Pattern mode:** Locks a fixed recoil curve.
- **Auto Parry:** Automates melee counterattacks.
- **Bunny Hop:** Enhanced jumping.
- **Perfect Active Reload:** Guaranteed reload hits.
- **Perfect Dashjump:** Improved dash mechanics.

### **⚠️ Notifications**
- **Boss Attack Alert:** Warns when a boss fight begins.

### **🎨 UI**
- **Drag & Drop:** Rearrange HUD elements freely.
- **Menu bind:** Change the menu key.
- **Background:** Toggle panel transparency.
